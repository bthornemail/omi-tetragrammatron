## Name And Mathematical Role

The name Tetragrammatron resolves in this framework as the fourfold governor: the Polybius/Miquel controller over FS, GS, RS, and US boundary directions.

The assignment is pattern-first. Tetragrammatron is not identified by the
decimal values of `0x3C` or `0x78`. It is identified by the operation that fits
the Polybius field: classify the gauge, compare complementary paths, fold a
candidate reading, and govern whether the resulting carry may proceed.

The visible four directions are:

```text

```

```text

```

The local hexadecimal Polybius gauge is:

```text

```

The tetrahedral diagonals are:

```text

```

Both close by sum:

```text

3 + 6 + 9 + C = 0x1E
```

So:


The full nibble field also closes:



The equal diagonal closures are checks on a deeper symmetry:


Tetragrammatron governs this fitted relation between distinct paths and common
closure. The sum `0x1E` records the agreement; it does not create the
governor.


Tetragrammatron governs this transition. It does not merely observe it. It decides whether a candidate crossing is lawful enough to carry forward.

The 16-bit sequence witnesses preserve operation order:


These two words contain the same two bands but in opposite order. They are not
Metatron alternators. They are Tetragrammatron operation traces:


The shift identity and the Polybius sum independently converge on `0x78`:


That convergence makes `0x78` a useful witness. It does not mean arithmetic

## Authority Boundary

Tetragrammatron may:

- validate OMI citation candidates
- produce validated Omi-Ring transition state
- write validated-state ring entries
- export and import validated-state ring memory
- compute deterministic folds over validated-state ring memory
- evaluate Polybius, Fano, BQF, chiral diagonal, and slot5040 witnesses

- parse Omi-Log source

## Validation And Storage Split

The implementation keeps validation and storage separate:

```text
validate candidate -> validated carry-forward state
store validated-state -> ring entry
```

Validation may validate or reject a candidate.

Storage records an already validated state.

Projection still waits for validated carry-forward state.
